# Gestures

## Pinch
Touch your thumb tip to your index tip. The ring under your fingers fills in
when handmedown sees the pinch. If it struggles, turn your palm toward the
camera.

## Tap
A pinch shorter than about a third of a second, without moving, is a tap.

## Throw
Let go while your hand is still moving. Cards glide, slow down, and bounce off
the edges of the screen.

## Stretch
Pinch the same card with both hands. Pull apart to grow it, push together to
shrink it.

## No camera?
The mouse works too: click is a pinch, Alt + scroll resizes. `Esc` clears the
board.
